07 · Hi, I'm Leah
Photo of Leah — or a favourite image

Hi, I'm Leah.

I've always loved being outdoors. There's something about nature, beautiful light, and noticing the little details around me that has always made me happy. Growing up in Far Rockaway, New York, where I still live today, that love naturally grew into a passion for photography.

When I got my first camera, I completely fell in love with it and just kept going. I upgraded my equipment, took courses, practiced constantly, and learned everything I could—not because I had to, but because I genuinely loved it.

Today, I get to photograph families and capture the warmth, connection, and personality that make each one special. I love creating beautiful portraits, but I also love the natural moments in between—the laughter, the little interactions, and the expressions that feel completely like you.

More than anything, I want your session to feel relaxed and enjoyable. Because when everyone feels comfortable and can simply be themselves, that's when the most meaningful photos happen.

Written from your own words — Far Rockaway, the outdoors, the first camera, and how much you love this. Read it out loud. It's the only part of the site written as you, so it has to sound like you talking. Change any word that doesn't.
